---
name: mainline-grill-me
description: Interview the user about a plan or design by resolving major decisions first, while supplying best-practice defaults for minor details unless challenged.
---

Interview me about the plan until we reach a shared understanding.

Focus on the main design branches first. Ask one major question at a time, and provide your recommended answer.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

For trivial or implementation-detail questions, do not ask one-by-one. List the best-practice default, give a short reason, and treat it as accepted unless I object.

Keep track of accepted decisions and use them to constrain later questions.
